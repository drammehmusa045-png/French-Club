About Nusrat French Club

Nusrat French Club is a French language and cultural club in The Gambia. This management system helps the club executives organize members, meetings, attendance and subscriptions in one place.

What We Manage

👥 Members

Keep records of every Nusrat French Club member, including their name, GR number and class.

📋 Attendance

Record members who attend every club meeting and keep their attendance history.

📅 Meetings

Create meetings and keep track of meeting dates and activities.

💰 Subscriptions

Track members who pay their club subscriptions and keep payment history.
