About Nusrat French Club

Nusrat French Club is a French language and cultural club in The Gambia. The club provides an environment where members can participate in French-related activities, meetings and learning experiences.

This website provides a digital management platform for Nusrat French Club. Club executives can manage members, organize meetings, record attendance and keep track of subscriptions in one place.

Our Purpose

🇫🇷 French Language

Support participation in French language activities and encourage members to develop their interest in French.

🌍 Culture

Encourage members to explore French-speaking cultures and take part in club activities.

🤝 Community

Provide a platform where French Club members can participate, connect and work together.

Nusrat French Club Management System

The management system helps the French Club executives organize important club records and activities through one central platform.

👥 Members

Keep records of Nusrat French Club members, including their name, GR number and class.

📋 Attendance

Record attendance for club meetings and maintain members' attendance history.

📅 Meetings

Create meetings and keep track of meeting dates, descriptions and club activities.

💰 Subscriptions

Record member subscriptions and maintain payment history.
